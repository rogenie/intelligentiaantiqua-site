The box is useful. It is also a draft machine, and the memory policy is yours to set. If you do not set it, the company already chose. The tool is <a href="/glossary/#super-intelligence">Super Intelligence (SI), still widely called AI</a>. ChatGPT keeps its product name.

People paste a contract, a school email, a password, a medical note. Then they treat the paragraph that comes back as if someone had checked it. Nobody had.

Two habits. Keep private text out. Check the reply against a page that is not the reply. The settings below are ChatGPT’s, from <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt">OpenAI’s data-controls article</a>. Another product will word the switch differently. Believe that product’s own page, not a guess.

## What should you leave out of the chat?

Leave out anything you would not want a stranger to read later: passwords, account numbers, medical records, and someone else’s private messages.

That is a user habit, and it matches a builder’s rule. The <a href="https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/guidelines-secure-ai-system-development">UK National Cyber Security Centre</a> tells organisations that build AI systems to make them work without revealing sensitive data to unauthorised parties. You are not the builder. You still choose what enters the box.

<a href="https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-protects-privacy/">OpenAI’s privacy note</a> says the same thing from the company side: do not share sensitive information you would not want used or reviewed. <a href="https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1">NIST’s Generative AI Profile</a> lists data privacy as a generative-AI risk, including leakage of health, biometric, location, and other sensitive data.

A harmless question is still fine. “Explain this public form in plain English” is a different act from pasting the form after you have filled in your bank details.

## What does ChatGPT do with the conversation?

On a personal Free, Plus, or Pro account, OpenAI says chats may be used to improve its models until you turn that setting off. Business, Enterprise, and Edu workspaces are not used for training by default.

The control is named “Improve the model for everyone.” <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt">OpenAI’s help page</a> says it is on by default for those personal plans. On the web, open your account menu, then Settings, then Data controls, then turn the switch off and confirm. On a phone, the same switch is under Settings, then Data controls. Turning it off does not delete the chat history. Saved chats stay saved.

<a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance">The companion article</a> says that after you opt out, new conversations are not used to improve the models. If you later tap thumbs-up or thumbs-down, OpenAI says the conversation tied to that feedback may still be used. A temporary chat is a separate choice: it is not used to improve the models, it does not appear in history, and it may be kept for up to 30 days for safety.

Plans change. If your screen does not match this paragraph, believe the help page over this one, and write to us if we have gone stale. The <a href="/corrections/">corrections page</a> is there for that.

## How do you check a reply before you use it?

Read the reply as a draft, then open a source that did not write it, and keep a claim only when that source supports it.

<a href="https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1">NIST</a> says confabulation is what happens when a generative system confidently states content that is wrong. Large language models predict the next word. A likely sentence is not a checked sentence. The <a href="https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/international-ai-safety-report-2026.pdf">International AI Safety Report 2026</a> says current systems sometimes fabricate information, produce flawed code, and give misleading advice, and that today’s methods do not bring the failure rate down to what many high-stakes settings need. The report’s name for trusting the output too quickly is <a href="/glossary/#automation-bias">automation bias</a>.

Use this order:

1. Leave secrets out of the box.
2. Set the training control for your plan, using the help page above.
3. If you might act on a sentence, open a primary source and compare. A legal, medical, or money decision needs a qualified person and the original document.

The assistant can still draft a packing list, rewrite a sentence you wrote, or walk you through a public page in plainer words. That is a good use of your time. The limit is the claim you would be sorry to get wrong. That one stays in your hands.
