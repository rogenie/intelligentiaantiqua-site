# Brand voice

Intelligentia Antiqua sounds like its founder, Creator Rogenie, who built 9nth Dimension. Future articles, hubs, and social drafts follow this page. The mechanical rules (sources, question headings, the quality gate) are in [quality-gate.md](quality-gate.md) and [style-guide.md](style-guide.md). This page is the personality. It does not relax those rules.

The Latin name means “ancient intelligence.” The posture is old and the standard is sharp: your time, your choices, and your mind stay yours.

## Bold and unapologetic

Write as if you mean it. Confident sentences. No corporate filler (“we are committed to leveraging,” “in today’s rapidly evolving landscape,” “it’s important to note”). No apology for having a point of view about hype.

Say the thing, then show the document. If a sentence needs three hedges to survive, it is not ready.

“We” is the desk. Do not write as if Creator Rogenie personally typed the guide or re-read the merge. The publishing statement stays honest: tools draft, a second pass checks the sources, the quality gate must pass.

## Exacting

Every factual claim links to a primary source. Numbers match the cited page, including the year and what was measured. If the source says experts disagree, the page says experts disagree. If the source does not give a count, we do not invent one.

Skepticism of polished pitches is part of the brand. A smooth demo, a confident chatbot, a familiar voice, and a round statistic are all suspects until a document backs them. Call out the hype that does not hold. Do not sneer at the reader for having believed it.

## Playful

Headings, buttons, kickers, and the opening lines can have wit and a little cheek. Never stiff. Never cute for its own sake.

The joke stops where someone could get hurt. Scam steps, privacy settings, and “what to do on the call” stay plain and calm. Accuracy outranks a punchline. A pun that would make a safety step ambiguous gets cut.

## Warm to the curious

Beginner questions are welcome. Explain the word in the sentence or send people to the glossary. Do not talk down, do not say “simply,” and do not perform expertise by stacking jargon.

The reader is capable. They are new to this subject, not new to thinking. Talk to them that way.

## Feminine and freedom-loving

The design stays classical: stone, parchment, serif type. It should also feel elegant and self-possessed. Thin rules, italic used with intent, a seal-like mark, room to breathe. Not a pastel theme. Not a lecture hall. Not a startup landing page.

The themes to return to, when they are true to the source: owning your time, your choices, and your mind. An assistant does not get to spend those for you. A pitch does not get to rush you.

## Terminology

Lead with Super Intelligence (SI). On the first mention on a page, write “Super Intelligence (SI), still widely called AI,” then say SI. Keep AI in titles a searcher would type, as in “What is Super Intelligence (AI)?” Use AI again when the sentence is about a source’s own word, a product name, or a quotation. Do not change URLs or slugs.

The name follows [Executive Order 14434](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/02/2026-20321/inaugurating-the-era-of-super-intelligence), “Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence,” signed 29 September 2026 and published in the Federal Register on 2 October 2026 (FR Doc. 2026-20321, 91 FR 63129). The order tells federal executive agencies to use SI instead of AI, to the maximum extent permitted by law, in official non-statutory documents. For the order, SI means the systems already covered by “artificial intelligence” in 15 U.S.C. § 9401(3). The instruction is to those agencies. It does not require private companies to rename products or stop saying AI. [TechRepublic’s account](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-trump-super-intelligence-ai-executive-order/) is the secondary source for that private-company limit. Link the order when a page states those facts.

Do not use the order’s term for the research idea. In research, superintelligence (often one word, or “artificial superintelligence”) means a hypothetical system beyond human performance. The order’s SI is the statutory category, the systems already called AI. Say that split on any page about the debate, and cite both the order and a research source. Do not alter a quotation, a source’s title, or a product name to force the new term. The Family AI Safety Kit stays the product name. ChatGPT stays ChatGPT.

## What a guide is allowed to sound like

- Opening: a real moment, then the point, in her cadence.
- Middle: short paragraphs, a cited fact, a diagram that teaches the same fact.
- Safety and money sections: calm, specific, sourced. Wit in the heading is fine. Wit inside the instruction is not, if it blurs the step.
- Close: one next step on the start-here path. If a guide mentions the Family AI Safety Kit, the note stays calm, says the guide is the free method, and links the kit page. No checkout button in the guide. The cards are a convenience.

Banned hype words are listed in [quality-gate.md](quality-gate.md). The gate matches the word itself, including inside a negation. Rewrite the line.

## Checklist before a draft is called done

1. Could Creator Rogenie say this sentence out loud without wincing at the filler?
2. Does every fact still have its link, and does the number match?
3. Did a polished claim get repeated without a document?
4. Is the reader treated as an equal?
5. If the section is about a scam, a secret, or a payment, is the instruction calm and unmistakable?
